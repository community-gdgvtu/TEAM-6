How RescueBite Works

A simple, secure 3-step pipeline to redirect edible food from landfill to families.

🍲
STEP 1

List Surplus Food

Restaurants & caterers post surplus prepared meals, bakery items, or produce with photos, quantity, and pickup times.

🤝
STEP 2

Verified NGO Reserves

Vetted non-profit organizations browse nearby available listings and reserve items for distribution with 1 click.

🌱
STEP 3

Pickup & Impact Recorded

NGOs collect the food, meals reach families in need, and verified impact analytics are logged on the platform.

⚡

Real-Time Surplus Exchange

Instant listing and atomic reservations ensure food is picked up while fresh and safe for consumption.

🛡️

Vetted & Verified Network

Every donor business and recipient NGO undergoes administrative verification to maintain quality and trust.

📊

Traceable Community Impact

Automated tracking of meals rescued, financial value saved, and environmental CO2 emissions prevented.
